### 2.1 Theoretical Differentiation and Scientific Positioning
1. **Vs. Javanmardi & Hüllermeier (2023)**: Javanmardi introduced split conformal prediction for RUL using symmetric absolute residuals. IntelliTwin extends this by introducing asymmetric dilation ($\gamma = 2, \beta = -\ln 2$) and discovering that while marginal coverage is preserved, conditional undercoverage persists in late wear stages.
2. **Vs. Diao et al. (2026)**: Diao evaluated LSTM quantile regression on C-MAPSS under symmetric metrics (PICP, MPIW). IntelliTwin connects uncertainty directly to sequential maintenance decisions, proving that interval width alone is insufficient to evaluate maintenance utility.
3. **Vs. Robinson (2026)**: Robinson embedded risk asymmetry into quantile loss functions during training. IntelliTwin applies post-hoc decision-aware conformal calibration to frozen base predictors, enabling distribution-free coverage without altering underlying neural representations.
4. **Vs. Cortes-Gomez et al. (2025)**: Cortes-Gomez established general utility-directed conformal prediction for static classification. IntelliTwin derives a temporal, non-stationary RUL formulation with continuous dilation across degradation lifecycles.
