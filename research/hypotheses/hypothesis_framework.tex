\begin{abstract}
This document presents the formal academic rendering of the IntelliTwin hypothesis framework, frozen numerical evaluation contract (\texttt{step06-v2}), statistical uncertainty rules, decision logic, outcome hierarchy, and evaluation criteria approved under ADR-018.
\textbf{Note:} The structured YAML specification in \url{research/hypotheses/hypothesis_framework.yaml} and the statistical protocol in \url{research/research_design/statistical_inference_protocol.md} serve as the canonical, authoritative sources of truth for all hypothesis definitions, metrics, comparators, numerical margins, and experimental mappings.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Governance Rules on Success Criteria and Protocol Integrity}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Success Criteria Rule:} The old percentage-based thresholds (5\%, 10\%, 15\%, 75\%) are permanently superseded. Each RQ possesses a pre-specified primary outcome, comparator set, frozen numerical practical-effect threshold, and cluster-bootstrap decision rule that classifies results as Supported, Unsupported, or Inconclusive under ADR-018.

\textbf{Outcome Hierarchy Rule:} The primary outcome determines the main conclusion for each research question. Secondary/supporting outcomes verify constraints, characterize trade-offs, and provide interpretation, but a favorable secondary outcome cannot replace failure on the primary outcome.

\textbf{No Post-Hoc Modification Rule:} No post-hoc modification of confirmatory evaluation criteria is permitted. Primary metrics, comparator sets, subgroup definitions, practical-effect thresholds, statistical decision rules, and outcome hierarchies must not be altered after observing confirmatory results. Any late modification must be documented as a protocol deviation and labelled exploratory.
\end{quote}

\section*{Numerical Evaluation Contract (ADR-018)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Protocol Version}: \texttt{step06-v2}
  \item \textbf{Primary Maintenance Scenario}: \texttt{primary\_reference} ($C_{\text{PM}} = 1.0, C_{\text{FAIL}} = 5.0, C_{\text{WASTE}} = 0.20$ SCU).
  \item \textbf{Secondary Sensitivity Scenarios}: \texttt{failure\_sensitive} ($C_{\text{FAIL}} = 10.0$) and \texttt{waste\_sensitive} ($C_{\text{WASTE}} = 0.40$).
  \item \textbf{Nominal Coverage}: Primary nominal coverage 90\% ($\alpha = 0.10$); secondary sensitivity levels at 80\% and 95\%.
  \item \textbf{Cost Improvement Margin}: $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU per engine (equal to the cost of one discarded cycle).
  \item \textbf{Coverage Error Improvement Margin}: $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$ (one percentage point).
  \item \textbf{Maximum Absolute Coverage Error Ceiling}: $E < 0.05$ (prespecified 85\%--95\% empirical acceptance band).
  \item \textbf{Interval Noninferiority Margins}: $D_{\text{width}} < 1.0$ cycle for MPIW and $D_{\text{score}} < 1.0$ cycle for Winkler interval score.
  \item \textbf{Robustness Non-Harm Margins}: $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > -0.20$ SCU per engine and $\Delta_{\text{error}} > -0.01$.
  \item \textbf{Failure Safeguard}: Observed fleet failure rate increase $\le 0.0$ against comparator.
  \item \textbf{Model Training Seeds}: Fixed five-seed estimand: \texttt{[2026, 2027, 2028, 2029, 2030]}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Statistical Uncertainty Framework}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Independent Unit}: The engine unit is the independent statistical sample unit. Individual sequence time windows or time steps extracted from the same physical engine exhibit strong temporal autocorrelation and must \textbf{NEVER} be treated as independent statistical observations.
  \item \textbf{Paired Comparisons}: Comparisons between the proposed method and baseline models evaluated on the same engine units or dataset splits are strictly paired.
  \item \textbf{Resampling Procedure}: 10,000 cluster-bootstrap resamples with replacement, stratified by split-generation lifetime quartiles ($Q_1$--$Q_4$), using PCG64 PRNG with \texttt{SeedSequence([2026, 6, dataset\_number])}.
  \item \textbf{Decision Intervals}: Confirmatory decision rules use \textbf{98.75\% cluster-bootstrap percentile confidence intervals} (quantiles 0.00625 and 0.99375), providing Bonferroni family-wise error control ($\alpha = 0.05 / 4 = 0.0125$) across the four research questions. Descriptive 95\% intervals (quantiles 0.025 and 0.975) are reported for context.
  \item \textbf{Intersection-Union Principle}: Each RQ requires all component conditions to pass at the decision level to achieve Supported classification.
  \item \textbf{Missing-Group Bound Propagation}: Replicate-level worst-group errors are recomputed across eligible groups ($\ge 10$ engines). For replicate $b$, if $|G_{\text{available}}^{(b)}| > 0$, $\text{WorstErr}_{\text{lower}}^{(b)} = \max_{g \in G_{\text{available}}^{(b)}} \text{Err}^{(b)}(g)$, else $0.0$; if $|G_{\text{missing}}^{(b)}| > 0$, $\text{WorstErr}_{\text{upper}}^{(b)} = \max(\text{WorstErr}_{\text{lower}}^{(b)}, 0.90)$, else $\text{WorstErr}_{\text{lower}}^{(b)}$. All-groups-missing yields exactly $[0.0, 0.90]$ with no $\max(\emptyset)$ error. Replicates are never discarded or redrawn.
  \item \textbf{Secondary P-Values}: No supplementary p-value hypothesis tests form part of the confirmatory protocol.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Global Decision Rule}

\begin{quote}
An RQ is \textbf{Supported} if and only if every required component condition passes using the 98.75\% decision intervals and observed failure safeguard; \textbf{Unsupported} if any required condition fails; and \textbf{Inconclusive} if any condition is indeterminate (e.g., CI overlaps the boundary) or degenerate.
\end{quote}

\section*{Formal Research Questions and Hypotheses}

\subsection*{Research Question 1: Sequential Maintenance Utility and Coverage Reliability}
\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=3.5cm]
  \item[Status:] Frozen (ADR-018)
  \item[Experiment:] \textbf{Experiment A} (FD002, \texttt{primary\_reference}, nominal 90\%)
  \item[Question:] Can a decision-aware, condition-adaptive conformal RUL calibration method improve sequential maintenance utility while preserving reliable prediction-interval coverage?
  \item[Null ($H_{0,1}$):] The proposed method does not improve maintenance utility by at least 0.20 SCU per engine over both global and standard condition-aware calibration, or fails coverage constraints.
  \item[Alt ($H_{1,1}$):] The proposed method improves maintenance utility by at least 0.20 SCU per engine over both global and standard condition-aware calibration while maintaining required coverage reliability.
  \item[Primary Outcome:] Total simulated maintenance cost per engine contrast ($\Delta_{\text{cost}}$)
  \item[Supporting Outcomes:] Marginal, worst-regime, and worst-stage coverage errors; fleet failure rate
  \item[Comparison:] Proposed method vs.\ Global Conformal Calibration and Standard Condition-Aware Calibration (both GBR and LSTM required).
  \item[Threshold:] $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU per engine; coverage error ceiling $E < 0.05$.
  \item[Supported Logic:] For both GBR and LSTM, lower CI of $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU against both baselines, upper CI of coverage errors $< 0.05$, and observed fleet failure rate increase $\le 0.0$.
  \item[Unsupported Logic:] For either model, upper CI of $\Delta_{\text{cost}} < 0.20$ SCU against either baseline, lower CI of any coverage error $> 0.05$, or observed failure rate increases.
  \item[Inconclusive Logic:] No condition fails, but at least one decision interval covers its boundary, or inference is degenerate.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Research Question 2: Conditional Reliability and Interval Sharpness}
\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=3.5cm]
  \item[Status:] Frozen (ADR-018)
  \item[Experiment:] \textbf{Experiment B} (FD002, nominal 90\%)
  \item[Question:] Can the proposed method improve reliability across operating regimes and degradation conditions without excessively widening prediction intervals?
  \item[Null ($H_{0,2}$):] The proposed method does not improve worst-group coverage error by at least 0.01 over both baselines, or causes interval width or score to deteriorate by 1.0 cycle or more.
  \item[Alt ($H_{1,2}$):] The proposed method improves worst-group coverage error by at least 0.01 over both baselines while restricting interval width and score deterioration to under 1.0 cycle.
  \item[Primary Outcome:] Worst-group conditional coverage error contrast ($\Delta_{\text{error}}$)
  \item[Supporting Outcomes:] MPIW contrast ($D_{\text{width}}$); interval score contrast ($D_{\text{score}}$); worst-group coverage error
  \item[Comparison:] Proposed method vs.\ Global Conformal and Standard Condition-Aware Calibration (both GBR and LSTM; both regimes and stages required).
  \item[Threshold:] $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$; worst-group error ceiling $E < 0.05$; $D_{\text{width}} < 1.0$ cycle; $D_{\text{score}} < 1.0$ cycle.
  \item[Supported Logic:] For both models and both subgroup families, lower CI of $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$ against both baselines, upper CI of worst-group error $< 0.05$, upper CI of $D_{\text{width}} < 1.0$ cycle, and upper CI of $D_{\text{score}} < 1.0$ cycle.
  \item[Unsupported Logic:] Upper CI of $\Delta_{\text{error}} < 0.01$, lower CI of worst-group error $> 0.05$, lower CI of $D_{\text{width}} > 1.0$ cycle, or lower CI of $D_{\text{score}} > 1.0$ cycle.
  \item[Inconclusive Logic:] No condition fails, but at least one decision interval covers its boundary, or inference is degenerate.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Research Question 3: Decision-Value Attribution vs. Matched-Conservatism Baselines}
\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=3.5cm]
  \item[Status:] Frozen (ADR-018)
  \item[Experiment:] \textbf{Experiment C} (FD002, \texttt{primary\_reference}, nominal 90\%)
  \item[Question:] Are the maintenance benefits of the proposed method attributable to decision-aware uncertainty calibration rather than simply more conservative prediction intervals or earlier maintenance?
  \item[Null ($H_{0,3}$):] The proposed method does not improve maintenance utility by at least 0.20 SCU per engine over every prespecified matched-conservatism control family, or violates coverage constraints.
  \item[Alt ($H_{1,3}$):] The proposed method improves maintenance utility by at least 0.20 SCU per engine over every prespecified matched-conservatism control family while preserving required coverage reliability.
  \item[Primary Outcome:] Maintenance cost improvement over matched-conservatism controls ($\Delta_{\text{cost}}$)
  \item[Supporting Outcomes:] Wasted Remaining Useful Life ($RUL_{\text{wasted}}$); cost-inefficient early PM rate; coverage error
  \item[Comparison:] Proposed method vs.\ all three prespecified control families (widened intervals, point-prediction safety margins, earlier-intervention policies).
  \item[Threshold:] $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU per engine against EVERY control family; coverage ceiling $E < 0.05$.
  \item[Supported Logic:] For both models, lower CI of $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU against every control family, upper CI of coverage errors $< 0.05$, and failure increase $\le 0.0$.
  \item[Unsupported Logic:] Upper CI of $\Delta_{\text{cost}} < 0.20$ SCU against any control family, lower CI of coverage error $> 0.05$, or failure rate increases.
  \item[Inconclusive Logic:] No condition fails, but at least one decision interval covers its boundary, or inference is degenerate.
  \item[Control Selection:] Control-family structure and parameter grids must be prespecified before confirmatory evaluation. Numerical control-parameter selection must use validation engines only; calibration engines must not be used to select or optimize matched-control parameters; test engines are untouched until confirmatory evaluation.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Research Question 4: Robustness and Generalization}
\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=3.5cm]
  \item[Status:] Frozen (ADR-018)
  \item[Experiment:] \textbf{Experiment D} (6 cells: FD001, FD002, FD004 $\times$ GBR, LSTM; weight $1/6$ per cell)
  \item[Question:] Does the proposed method retain its calibration and maintenance-decision benefits across operating conditions, base RUL predictors, datasets, and repeated experimental runs?
  \item[Null ($H_{0,4}$):] The proposed method fails to achieve aggregate improvements ($\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU, $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$) across the evaluation matrix, or exhibits material harm ($\Delta_{\text{cost}} < -0.20$ SCU or $\Delta_{\text{error}} < -0.01$) in any cell.
  \item[Alt ($H_{1,4}$):] The proposed method achieves aggregate improvements across the evaluation matrix without material harm in any individual configuration cell.
  \item[Primary Outcome:] Weighted aggregate contrasts and cell-level non-harm bounds
  \item[Supporting Outcomes:] Individual cell effect sizes; cell-level decision intervals
  \item[Comparison:] Proposed method vs.\ Global Conformal and Standard Condition-Aware Calibration across 6 cells.
  \item[Threshold:] Aggregate: $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU, $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$; Cell-level non-harm: $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > -0.20$ SCU, $\Delta_{\text{error}} > -0.01$.
  \item[Supported Logic:] Lower CI of weighted aggregate $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > 0.20$ SCU, lower CI of weighted aggregate $\Delta_{\text{error}} > 0.01$, lower CI of cell $\Delta_{\text{cost}} > -0.20$ SCU in every cell, lower CI of cell $\Delta_{\text{error}} > -0.01$ in every cell, and all cell safeguards hold.
  \item[Unsupported Logic:] Upper CI of aggregate $\Delta_{\text{cost}} < 0.20$ SCU, upper CI of aggregate $\Delta_{\text{error}} < 0.01$, upper CI of cell $\Delta_{\text{cost}} < -0.20$ SCU or $\Delta_{\text{error}} < -0.01$ in any cell, or any cell safeguard fails.
  \item[Inconclusive Logic:] No condition fails, but at least one decision interval covers its boundary, or inference is degenerate.
\end{description}
