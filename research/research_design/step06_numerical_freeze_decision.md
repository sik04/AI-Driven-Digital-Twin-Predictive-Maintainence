## 2. Deterministic Validation Pilot Results

The validation-only decision pilot was executed on the 26 VALIDATION engines from `data/splits/fd002_engine_split_seed_2026.json`. Zero held-out test engines were inspected or used.

Decision states were generated from cycle 30 through $T-1$. The pilot policy fixture was defined as `MAINTAIN_NOW` when `lower_rul <= 5.0`.

### Interpretation
- **PERFECT**: Demonstrates ideal timing under oracle predictions; policy maintains exactly when lower bound reaches 5.0 cycles, yielding zero failures and a deterministic wasted RUL of 5.0 cycles.
- **OPTIMISTIC**: Demonstrates failure behavior when predictions systematically overestimate remaining life; policy continues past cycle $T-1$, terminating in functional failure at cycle $T$ across all 26 engines.
- **PESSIMISTIC**: Demonstrates premature maintenance when predictions underestimate remaining life; policy triggers maintenance early, discarding an average of 30.0 usable operational cycles per engine.
