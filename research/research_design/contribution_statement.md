# Research Contribution

## Targeted Novelty

"A decision-aware, condition-adaptive conformal calibration method for RUL prediction that explicitly incorporates sequential maintenance utility while preserving reliable conditional uncertainty coverage."

## Scientific Position

The proposed contribution is METHOD-LEVEL integration.

The project is NOT claiming novelty for the individual ideas of:
- conformal prediction
- RUL uncertainty quantification
- condition-aware calibration
- regime-aware calibration
- conditional coverage analysis
- uncertainty-aware maintenance
- maintenance-cost optimization
- Digital Twins for predictive maintenance

These areas already have prior research documented in our systematic literature review.

## Exact Contribution Being Investigated

The proposed study aims to jointly combine:
1. RUL prediction
2. condition-aware uncertainty calibration
3. conformal reliability
4. sequential maintenance consequences
5. maintenance utility/cost
6. explicit controls against simple over-conservatism

The key scientific question is whether calibration can be made decision-aware while still retaining meaningful reliability properties.

## Closest-Work Boundary

Existing research has already studied overlapping components separately or in partial combinations. Therefore, we avoid absolute novelty claims such as "first ever", "completely novel", "no previous study", or "unprecedented".

Instead, we state:
"To the best of the verified literature review, we have not identified prior RUL work that jointly formulates condition-aware conformal calibration around downstream sequential maintenance utility while preserving reliability constraints."

This claim remains dependent on the completeness of the closest-work audit documented in `research/literature/closest_work_matrix.csv`.

## Contribution Classification

- **Primary contribution**: Proposed method
- **Supporting contributions**:
  - controlled comparative evaluation
  - conditional reliability analysis
  - maintenance-decision evaluation
  - robustness analysis
  - reproducible benchmark implementation
