# Phase 8 Exploratory Analysis: Scientific Limitations

1. **Exploratory Post-Confirmatory Protocol**: This analysis was executed post-confirmatory. All statistical intervals and contrasts are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.
2. **Synthetic Stress Probes**: Sensor noise, causal dropout, and setting-bias levels are predefined synthetic engineering probes designed to stress-test numerical boundaries, not physically calibrated failure models of turbofan engines.
3. **No Recalibration or Winning Model Selection**: Phase 8 findings are observational. No new model weights were trained, no hyperparameter tuning was conducted, and the production calibrator registry was not updated.
4. **Population Heterogeneity**: Engine-level degradation distributions differ significantly across C-MAPSS sub-corpora. Aggregate descriptive means must not be interpreted as inferential population parameters.
