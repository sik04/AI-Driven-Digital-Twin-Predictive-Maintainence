# Phase 8 Exploratory Analysis: Scientific Limitations

1. **Exploratory Post-Confirmatory Scope**: This protocol was designed after inspection of the Step 9 held-out confirmatory results. Findings must be interpreted as exploratory and hypothesis-generating, not confirmatory proof.
2. **Synthetic Engineering Probes**: The sensor noise, dropout, and setting-bias levels are predefined engineering stress probes, not calibrated physical models of real-world turbofan sensor failure dynamics.
3. **Unadjusted Multiplicity**: The 95% bootstrap intervals reported in Phase 8 are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. They quantify sampling variability across engines under specific experimental conditions without formal FWER guarantees.
4. **No Recalibration of Production System**: Findings from Phase 8 are observational; they do not alter or update the frozen production models or calibrator registries.
