\section{Related Work and Positioning}

\subsection{Prognostic Models and Asymmetric Penalties}
Prognostic modeling for aero-engines widely benchmarked on C-MAPSS~\cite{saxena2008damage} encompasses deep architectures including recurrent neural networks and gradient-boosted decision trees. While the C-MAPSS scoring metric penalizes overestimation ($d > 0$) more severely than underestimation ($d < 0$), base models are typically trained under symmetric loss functions.

\subsection{Conformal Uncertainty Quantification}
Conformal prediction~\cite{javanmardi2023conformal} provides distribution-free marginal guarantees. Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) has been applied to aero-engines by Diao et al.~\cite{diao2026turbofan} and Robinson~\cite{robinson2026riskaware}. However, standard split conformal methods assume observation exchangeability. In degradation trajectories, observations are clustered within engines; Lee, Barber, and Willett~\cite{lee2023hierarchical} established hierarchical conformal inference, placing $\frac{1}{m+1}$ probability mass at $+\infty$ to target valid coverage for unseen clusters under exchangeable group sampling.

IntelliTwin uses an engine-weighted conformal construction motivated by hierarchical conformal prediction. Formal coverage results require the hierarchical sampling and exchangeability assumptions stated in the underlying theory. Equal engine weighting does not establish these assumptions for ordered, lifetime-stratified degradation trajectories. This study therefore evaluates marginal and subgroup coverage empirically. It does not claim guaranteed coverage for each engine, lifecycle stage, operating regime, complete trajectory, or adaptively selected maintenance time. Nominal 90% coverage is a target, not a guaranteed realized coverage proportion. Maintenance outcomes are evaluated separately under the frozen simulator.

\subsection{Subgroup Diagnostics and Maintenance Utility}
Auditing localized reliability under operating-regime shifts~\cite{yang2026empirical} and lifecycle degradation stages~\cite{benabdennour2026grouped} demonstrates that marginal coverage frequently conceals localized failure in high-stress regimes. Concurrently, utility-directed conformal prediction~\cite{cortesgomez2025utility} demonstrated the benefits of decision-aware nonconformity scores. IntelliTwin unites hierarchical engine-weighted conformal inference with asymmetric decision-aware score functions evaluated in sequential maintenance simulation.
