\section{Limitations, Threats to Validity, and Conclusion}

\subsection{Research Limitations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Benchmark Simulation}: Evaluations are conducted on synthetic NASA C-MAPSS data where degradation is monotonic. Operational aircraft engines experience maintenance reversibility and non-engine sensor faults not captured in the benchmark.
  \item \textbf{Synthetic Economic Scenarios}: The Simulated Cost Units ($\CPM=1.0, \CFAIL=5.0, \CWASTE=0.20$) represent study-defined benchmark preferences, not empirical airline accounting constants.
  \item \textbf{Conformal Theory Boundaries}: IntelliTwin uses an engine-weighted conformal construction motivated by hierarchical conformal prediction. Formal coverage results require the hierarchical sampling and exchangeability assumptions stated in the underlying theory. Equal engine weighting does not establish these assumptions for ordered, lifetime-stratified degradation trajectories. This study therefore evaluates marginal and subgroup coverage empirically. It does not claim guaranteed coverage for each engine, lifecycle stage, operating regime, complete trajectory, or adaptively selected maintenance time. Nominal 90% coverage is a target, not a guaranteed realized coverage proportion. Maintenance outcomes are evaluated separately under the frozen simulator.
  \item \textbf{Research Prototype Boundaries}: The system is an offline, trace-driven prognostic decision-support research prototype; physical engine integration and operational airline validation have not been demonstrated.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Current Status and Conclusion}
Base predictors and conformal calibrators are implemented and frozen. The committed v4 pre-test audit reports PASS and PRETEST\_READY. Held-out execution has not started, and RQ1–RQ4 outcomes remain pending. Development validation and audit results do not establish confirmatory maintenance benefit, subgroup reliability, or robustness.

The reviewed repository baseline is commit \texttt{c163bb279e7427b8f1b6e75955af4969ff9370fd}, with current committed readiness evidence in \texttt{experiments/pretest\_verification\_v4/}. All methodology, base models, calibrator bundles, and evaluation contracts are completely frozen and audited. Upon completion of the official held-out test evaluation, verified empirical results will be incorporated to resolve RQ1--RQ4.
