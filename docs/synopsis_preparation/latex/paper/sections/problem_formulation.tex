\section{Problem Formulation and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:problem_formulation}

\subsection{Trajectory Formulation, Causal Information Horizon and RUL Target Definitions}
Let $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$ denote an engine unit, and let $t \in \{1, \dots, T_i\}$ denote operational flight cycles, where $T_i$ is the cycle of total failure. At each cycle $t$, an engine produces raw telemetry $x_{it} \in \mathbb{R}^{17}$ comprising 3 operating settings and 14 qualified degradation sensors.

Causal sliding windows of length $W = 30$ cycles with stride 1 and strictly no padding are extracted:
\begin{equation}
X_{it} = [x_{i, t-29}, \; x_{i, t-28}, \; \dots, \; x_{it}] \in \mathbb{R}^{30 \times 17}
\end{equation}
Qualified inference endpoints are $V_i = \{30, 31, \dots, T_i\}$, yielding $n_i = T_i - 29$ valid predictions.

Under ADR-012, two distinct target horizons are formalized:
\begin{align}
\cappedRUL(t) &= \min\left(125, \; T_i - t\right) \quad \text{(Capped Training Target)} \\
\trueRUL(t) &= T_i - t \quad \text{(Uncapped Evaluation \& Calibration Target)}
\end{align}
Base models are optimized on $\cappedRUL$ as a modeling convention. Calibration nonconformity scores and downstream maintenance simulations evaluate true uncapped longevity $\trueRUL$.

\subsection{Conformal Prediction Intervals}
Given a base predictor point estimate $p_{it} = \max(0.0, \; \hat{y}(X_{it}))$, a conformal calibrator constructs a prediction interval:
\begin{equation}
C(X_{it}) = [L_{it}, \; U_{it}]
\end{equation}
such that empirical marginal coverage targets nominal coverage $1 - \alpha$. In this study, nominal coverage is set to 90\% ($\alpha = 0.10$), with secondary sensitivities at 80\% and 95\%. Nominal 90\% coverage is a target, not a guaranteed realized coverage proportion.

\subsection{The Prespecified Hypothesis Framework (ADR-018)}
The study evaluates four prespecified hypotheses governed by the following operational decision logic:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RQ1 (Maintenance Utility)}:
    \begin{align*}
      H_{0,1}&: \Deltacost \le 0.20~\SCU \text{ or } E_{\text{coverage}} \ge 0.05 \text{ or } \Deltafail > 0.0 \\
      H_{1,1}&: \Deltacost > 0.20~\SCU \text{ and } E_{\text{coverage}} < 0.05 \text{ and } \Deltafail \le 0.0
    \end{align*}
    evaluated for both GBR and LSTM on FD002 against both \texttt{global\_hcp} and \texttt{regime\_hcp} under \texttt{primary\_reference} at 90\% coverage, requiring cost improvement, marginal, worst-regime and worst-stage absolute coverage error ceilings ($< 0.05$), and observed failure safeguards.
  \item \textbf{RQ2 (Subgroup Reliability)}:
    \begin{align*}
      H_{0,2}&: \Deltaerror \le 0.01 \text{ or } E_{\text{worst}} \ge 0.05 \text{ or } \Dwidth \ge 1.0 \text{ or } \Dscore \ge 1.0 \\
      H_{1,2}&: \Deltaerror > 0.01 \text{ and } E_{\text{worst}} < 0.05 \text{ and } \Dwidth < 1.0 \text{ and } \Dscore < 1.0
    \end{align*}
    evaluated for both models, both baselines, and both subgroup families (operating regimes $C_1$--$C_6$ and lifecycle stages Early/Mid/Late), requiring worst-group error improvement, absolute worst-group error ceilings, and interval width/score noninferiority.
  \item \textbf{RQ3 (Decision-Value Attribution)}:
    \begin{align*}
      H_{1,3}&: \Deltacost > 0.20~\SCU, \; E_{\text{coverage}} < 0.05, \text{ and } \Deltafail \le 0.0 \\
      &\quad \text{over \textbf{all four} prespecified matched controls}
    \end{align*}
    evaluated for both models against matched-width intervals, widened intervals, point safety margins, and earlier interventions, requiring cost improvement, proposed-method coverage ceilings, and observed failure safeguards against every control.
  \item \textbf{RQ4 (Generalization \& Non-Harm)}:
    \begin{align*}
      H_{1,4}&: \Deltacost^{\text{agg}} > 0.20~\SCU, \; \Deltaerror^{\text{agg}} > 0.01, \text{ cell non-harm, and safeguards}
    \end{align*}
    evaluated across the balanced 6-cell matrix (FD001, FD002, FD004 $\times$ GBR, LSTM) weighted equally ($1/6$ each), requiring aggregate cost and subgroup-error improvements against both baselines, every-cell non-harm ($\Deltacost > -0.20~\SCU$, $\Deltaerror > -0.01$), and all cell-level coverage, width, score, and observed-failure safeguards.
\end{itemize}
