\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Remaining Useful Life Point Prognostics}
Prognostic health management of aero-engines was popularized by the NASA C-MAPSS dataset release~\cite{saxena2008damage}, which formalized run-to-failure simulation across varied operational flight conditions and fault modes. Standard point predictors model RUL using deep neural networks or gradient-boosted decision trees. While the C-MAPSS asymmetric scoring metric heavily penalizes overestimation ($d > 0$), base models are routinely optimized under symmetric loss objectives. Furthermore, point predictions provide no confidence intervals, depriving operators of actionable risk metrics.

\subsection{Conformal Uncertainty Quantification in PHM}
Conformal prediction~\cite{javanmardi2023conformal} has emerged as an authoritative distribution-free uncertainty framework. Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) was adapted to aero-engines by Diao et al.~\cite{diao2026turbofan} using LSTM networks on C-MAPSS FD001 and FD002, and by Robinson~\cite{robinson2026riskaware} across all four C-MAPSS subsets. However, standard split conformal methods assume observation exchangeability. Because sensor telemetry is autocorrelated within an engine, pooling raw cycles violates exchangeability and over-represents long-lived engines. Lee, Barber, and Willett~\cite{lee2023hierarchical} proved that for clustered hierarchical data, finite empirical mass must be bounded by $\frac{m}{m+1}$, with the remaining $\frac{1}{m+1}$ probability mass placed at $+\infty$, using engine-weighted quantiles to guarantee finite-sample coverage for unseen clusters.

\subsection{Conditional Reliability Diagnostics and Maintenance Economics}
Recent studies emphasize that marginal coverage obscures severe conditional vulnerabilities. Yang et al.~\cite{yang2026empirical} audited empirical calibration across operating-regime shifts in rotating bearings, revealing acute localized undercoverage. BenAbdennour~\cite{benabdennour2026grouped} demonstrated grouped validation and interval-safety calibration across degradation domains, showing non-linear expansion of uncertainty during terminal wear stages. 

Concurrently, uncertainty-aware maintenance scheduling has been investigated via simulation and reinforcement learning~\cite{chen2022data,zhu2025predictive,walia2026uncertainty}. Cortes-Gomez et al.~\cite{cortesgomez2025utility} introduced Utility-Directed Conformal Prediction (UDCP), demonstrating that tailoring nonconformity scores to downstream decision utility substantially reduces operational loss. IntelliTwin synthesizes these domains, evaluating whether asymmetric decision-aware score inversion can improve sequential maintenance utility while preserving subgroup reliability and coverage safety.
