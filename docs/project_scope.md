# Project Scope: IntelliTwin Research Framework

## 1. Executive Summary

**IntelliTwin** is a scientific research initiative investigating AI-driven Digital Twin architectures for the predictive maintenance (PdM) of critical engineering assets. The project is conducted jointly by **Mayank Singh**, **Shiksha Pandey**, and **Ruchi Gupta**.

The primary objective of this project is high-quality, publishable scholarly research supported by reproducible empirical evidence. While engineering software and an interactive digital twin representation are integral components of the research deliverable, software artifacts serve as implementations of tested scientific hypotheses rather than isolated commercial applications.

---

## 2. Research Focus and Objectives

The investigation centers on addressing three foundational challenges in engineering prognostics:

1. **Robust Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Estimation**:
   - Evaluating degradations across varying operational profiles and noisy sensor regimes using consistent, engine-level evaluation protocols.
2. **Rigorous Uncertainty Quantification (UQ)**:
   - Quantifying epistemic and aleatoric uncertainties to ensure prognostic confidence intervals are statistically sound, reliable, and well-calibrated.
3. **Uncertainty-Aware Maintenance Decision Support**:
   - Formulating decision-making mechanisms that translate calibrated prognostic distributions into operational warning triggers and scheduled interventions, outperforming static heuristics.
4. **Digital Twin Architectural Synchronization**:
   - Designing bidirectional state reflection and telemetry simulation between the physical asset and its digital prognostic counterpart.

---

## 3. Scope Boundaries and Governance

To maintain scientific integrity, the project is governed by strict boundaries across project phases:

- **Phase 0 (Current)**: Establishes repository governance, reproducibility protocols, dependency management, baseline templates, and quality verification.
- **Phase 1 (Upcoming)**: Executes a systematic literature review following PRISMA-style guidelines. No model development or empirical claims occur prior to literature synthesis.
- **Phases 2–12**: Proceed sequentially through hypothesis formalization, benchmark protocol formulation, baseline benchmarking, novel model investigation, uncertainty calibration, digital twin prototype construction, and peer-reviewed manuscript submission.

---

## 4. Academic Integrity and Publication Standards

1. **No Fabricated Evidence**: Literature findings, performance metrics, and comparison baselines must be derived from verified papers or reproducible local runs.
2. **Transparent Reporting**: Model limitations, failure modes, computational overhead, and boundary conditions must be analyzed and reported with equal prominence to positive results.
3. **Traceability**: Every published result or claim must trace back to a specific experiment record, random seed, dataset version, and Git commit SHA.
